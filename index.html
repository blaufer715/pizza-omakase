About

From New York to Israel

I grew up in New York and ate pizza almost every day as a kid. That New York pizza is what inspired me, and it's what I want to bring to Israel.

After making aliyah, I went to culinary school at Danon in Tel Aviv, where my passion for cooking and flavor kept growing.

It started with experimenting with pizza at home. More and more friends heard about it and asked to join, and it grew into something bigger: Pizza Omakase.
