About the Chef

From New York to Israel

I grew up in New York and ate pizza almost every day as a kid. That New York pizza is what inspired me, and it's what I want to bring to Israel.

After making aliyah, I went to culinary school at Danon in Tel Aviv, where my passion for cooking and flavor kept growing.

It started with experimenting with pizza at home. More and more friends heard about it and asked to join, and it grew into something bigger: Pizza Omakase.

What is Pizza Omakase?

Pizza Omakase is an exciting experience where I make 5 unique pies fresh for my guests. Each evening begins with wine and an appetizer to welcome my guests, followed by 5 pizzas in a row, each with different flavors, toppings or pizza styles. The evening finishes with a sweet treat to cap off the night.

It's one of the most unique pizza experiences in the world, and I hope you will join.

Additional Information

Please note: All pizzas are made in a kosher kitchen with kosher ingredients. As such, there will be no meat or poultry products served.
